## Company Overview
Marlow & Finch designs and sells home, kitchen, and lifestyle products through three channels — 42 company-owned retail stores, a DTC e-commerce site, and wholesale accounts with ~310 doors (department stores and specialty chains). Products are organized into five categories: Kitchen, Dining, Textiles, Décor, and Storage & Organization. The company has historically competed on quality and in-store experience rather than price, and Q4 (holiday) typically drives 34–38% of annual revenue.

## What the Data Shows
- **Revenue**: Total revenue declined year-over-year in every quarter of FY25 versus FY24. Q4 FY25 revenue was $55.5M vs $59.2M in Q4 FY24.
- **Gross margin**: Fell from 52.4% (Q1 FY24) to 44.9% (Q4 FY25) — a 7.5 point drop over two years.
- **By channel (Q4 FY25 vs Q4 FY24)**: Retail -11.7%, Wholesale -17.0%, DTC +8.8%.
- **Store fleet health**: Comparable store sales worsened from -2.1% to -11.7%, traffic down 14.3%, and stores below four-wall profitability rose from 5 to 11 (out of 42).
- **Category mix**: Décor (22% of revenue) is the weakest category — down 19% YoY, lowest margin (39.5%), lowest sell-through (44%). Storage & Organization is the strongest — up 11% YoY, 82% sell-through, but stocking out on top SKUs.
- **Inventory**: Total inventory is 22% above plan, with 24% of it aged over 180 days. Markdown rate rose from 23% to 31%. Stockouts are estimated to cost $2.1M in lost sales per quarter.

## Issues Flagged by Leadership
The brief lists six issues raised by the CEO, CFO, and VP Merchandising — each stated as a hypothesis to test, not a confirmed fact:
1. Revenue decline across Retail and Wholesale
2. Margin erosion
3. Inventory imbalance
4. Store fleet underperformance
5. Décor category weakness
6. Wholesale account attrition
