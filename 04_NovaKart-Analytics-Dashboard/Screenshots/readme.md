
# 🖼 Screenshots

Page-by-page exports of the NovaKart Analytics Dashboard, in navigation order. Each image is a full-resolution capture of the live Power BI report.

## Files

### `Home.png`
Landing page. Dark sidebar with project title, a short business-framing statement, key project stats (records cleaned, revenue analyzed, questions answered), and a numbered "Contents" list linking to each of the 5 report pages.

### `Executive_Summary.png`
**Page 1.** Six top-level KPI cards (Net Revenue, Order Count, AOV, Gross Profit, Margin %, Cancellation Rate %), a monthly net revenue trend line chart spanning Jan 2024–Dec 2025, a category revenue bar chart, and three supporting panels: top 5 products, revenue-by-channel donut, and top 5 cities.

### `Sales_Analysis.png`
**Page 2.** Six KPI cards (Net Revenue, YoY Growth %, Avg Discount %, Gross Profit, Top 20% Customer Revenue Share, Oct–Nov Revenue Share %), a margin-by-sub-category bar chart, top 5 states table, a segment-vs-revenue donut, a combined category revenue/margin table, and orders-by-payment-method bar chart.
 
### `Delivery_Return_Insight.png`
**Page 3.** Six KPI cards (Net Revenue, Cancelled Revenue, Total Returns, Return Rate %, Avg Delivery Days, Worst Category), a return-rate-by-sub-category heat-shaded table, top 10 return reasons, Metro vs Tier-2 delivery comparison, channel-vs-delivery-days chart, delivery-speed-vs-return-rate table, payment-type-vs-cancellation table, and city-vs-COD table.

### `Customer_Analysis.png`
**Page 4.** Six KPI cards (Total Customers, Repeated Customers, COD %, Metro vs Tier-2 Delivery Gap, Repeat Customer %, Top 20% Revenue Share %), a segment comparison table with the Premium-vs-New AOV gap called out, a discount-band-vs-AOV table with an "As Discount Increases AOV Decreases" insight callout, top 10 customers by revenue, a channel-vs-orders donut, a repeated-vs-one-time-customers table, and the full channel performance table (discount, margin, return rate, delivery, all in one view).

### `Recommendation.png`
**Page 5.** The Q40 founder recommendation, structured as Problem Identified → Recommendation → Track, followed by six supporting KPI cards and a full channel comparison table backing up the Marketplace finding.

## Purpose

These screenshots are used inline throughout the [main project README](../README.md), placed directly next to the written findings for each page. They also serve as a no-software-required way to review the dashboard's design and content without opening Power BI or the PDF export.
