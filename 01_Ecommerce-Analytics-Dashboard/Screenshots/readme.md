# Dashboard Screenshots

Six pages, each mapped to a specific purpose or business question.

## 1. Home
![Home](1.%20Home.png)

Landing page with navigation cards linking to all 6 report pages: Executive Summary, Revenue & Profit Analysis, Customer Insights, Return Order Insights, Marketing & Seasonal Analysis, and Conclusion.

## 2. Executive Overview
![Executive Overview](2.%20Executive%20Overview.png)

**KPIs:** Total Revenue (236.76K), Total Profit (66.19K), Total Orders (1,000), Total Customers (326), Profit Margin % (27.96%), Return Rate % (8.20%)

High-level snapshot of the business: monthly revenue & profit trend, revenue by country, revenue & profit by region, and a country/city performance table. Sets the context before drilling into specific questions on later pages.

## 3. Revenue & Profit Analysis
![Revenue & Profit Analysis](3.%20Revenue%20&%20Profit%20Analysis.png)

**Answers Business Question 1:** Which categories and products drive revenue vs. profit, and where is discounting causing losses?

Includes a Field Parameter toggle to switch a chart between Total Revenue and Total Profit by sub-category, profit by category and payment method, top 5 / bottom 5 products by profit, and a profit-by-discount-bucket chart showing profit turns negative once discount exceeds ~20%.

---

## 4. Customer Analysis
![Customer Analysis](4.%20Customer%20Analysis.png)

**Answers Business Question 2:** Who are our highest-value customers, and how much revenue comes from repeat vs. one-time buyers?

Shows customers by payment method, customer type (Repeat: 275 vs. One-time: 51), customer segment breakdown (Consumer/Corporate/Home Office), average order value by segment, and revenue split by customer type (95.2% from repeat customers).

---

## 5. Return Orders Insight
![Return Orders Insight](5.%20Return%20Orders.png)

**Answers Business Question 3:** What's driving returns, and which categories or shipping modes hurt net profit?

Return rate % by category (Fashion highest at 14.16%), profit vs. loss-profit by category, total profit vs. loss-profit split, return rate % by shipping mode, monthly orders vs. returned orders trend, and a shipping-mode performance table (orders, return rate, delivery days, shipping cost, profit margin).

---

## 6. Marketing & Seasonal Analysis
![Marketing & Seasonal Analysis](6%20Marketing%20&%20Seasonal%20Insights.png)

**Answers Business Question 4:** Which marketing channel acquires the most valuable orders, and how should Q4 seasonality shape planning?

Revenue & profit by marketing channel (Organic Search leads), average order value by channel and segment, weekday vs. weekend performance, quarterly revenue trend by channel, and channel performance by customer count.
